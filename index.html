Frequently Asked Questions

Does Doodh Diary work offline? +
Yes. It is designed to work without internet. Your entries and bills stay on your device.
Can I use the app without internet? +
Yes! DoodhDiary works completely offline. Your data is stored locally on your device and can be synced to cloud when internet is available.
Is there a login? +
We use a quick 4‑digit PIN (no OTP) so families can access their own profiles easily.
Can I share the monthly bill? +
Yes. Export a clean PNG and send via WhatsApp or save to gallery.
Which languages are supported? +
English and Hindi from day one. More languages planned.
Is my data secure? +
We use end-to-end encryption and your data is stored securely. We never share your personal information with third parties.
